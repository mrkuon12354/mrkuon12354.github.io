Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Auto Post does not have a server and does not keep any user data online. All data (login tokens and the connected account names) is stored only on the owner's own computer.

How to delete your data

  • In the app: open "Connect accounts" and click "Disconnect" for YouTube, Facebook or TikTok. The stored tokens are deleted immediately.
  • Google / YouTube: remove "Auto Post" at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Facebook: go to Settings & privacy > Settings > Business integrations, select "Auto Post" and click "Remove".
  • TikTok: go to Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions, select "Auto Post" and click "Remove access".

Request deletion by email

If you believe Auto Post holds any data about you, email mrkuon12354@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". The request will be handled within 30 days.

Auto Post is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Google, YouTube, Meta, Facebook or TikTok.
