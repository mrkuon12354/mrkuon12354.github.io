Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. About the app

Auto Post is a personal tool used only by its owner, mrkuon12354, to upload the owner's own videos and posts to the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

2. Data the app uses

The app only uses the owner's own data:

  • Login tokens (access tokens and refresh tokens) created when the owner signs in with Google, Facebook or TikTok.
  • Basic account information of the owner: the YouTube channel name, the Facebook Page name and ID, and the TikTok display name and avatar. This is used only to show which account is connected.
  • YouTube playlists of the owner's channel: the playlist names are read so the owner can choose one, and new videos are added to the chosen playlist.
  • The owner's own video files, images and captions, stored on the owner's computer.

3. Data the app does NOT collect

The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of other people. It has no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no tracking.

4. Where data is stored

All data is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sent to any server other than the official APIs of Google (YouTube), Meta (Facebook) and TikTok. The app does not have its own server.

5. How data is used

The data is used only to upload and schedule the owner's own content on the owner's own accounts. It is not used for any other purpose and is never transferred to third parties.

6. Google API Services

Auto Post's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Auto Post uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Removing access and deleting data

The owner can disconnect each account at any time inside the app, which deletes the stored tokens, or revoke access in the platform's settings:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Facebook: Settings & privacy > Settings > Business integrations
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions

See Data Deletion for details.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. The latest version is always available at this page.
